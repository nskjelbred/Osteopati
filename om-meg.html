Bakgrunn og utdanning

Osteopat (Noreg & Sveits), Doctor of Physical Therapy (USA)

Ivar Otto Skjelbred har djup fagleg forankring i manuell medisin og har teke vidareutdanning i biodynamisk osteopati i USA. Ivar har studert under framståande lærarar som Dr. Viola Frymann ved Osteopathic Center for Children i San Diego – eit klinisk og undervisande senter i samarbeid med Western University of Health Sciences – samt Dr. James Jealous, ein leiande autoritet innan tradisjonell osteopati og «Osteopathy in the Cranial Field».

Ivar er utdanna Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) i USA, og autorisert osteopat D.O. i både Noreg og Sveits. Med lang klinisk erfaring kombinerer Ivar eit respektfullt og lyttande møte med pasienten med djup innsikt i kroppens sjølvhelingsprosessar – i tråd med dei opphavlege prinsippa i osteopatisk medisin.

Background and education

Doctor of Physical Therapy (USA), Osteopath D.O. (Norway & Switzerland)

Ivar Otto Skjelbred has a solid foundation in manual medicine and has completed advanced training in biodynamic osteopathy in the United States. He studied under leading figures such as Dr. Viola Frymann at the Osteopathic Center for Children in San Diego – a clinical and educational center in collaboration with Western University of Health Sciences – as well as Dr. James Jealous, a key authority in traditional osteopathy and Osteopathy in the Cranial Field.

Ivar holds a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) degree from the USA and is a licensed osteopath D.O. in both Norway and Switzerland. With many years of clinical experience, Ivar combines a respectful, attentive approach with deep insight into the body’s innate self-healing processes – always in line with the original principles of osteopathic medicine.
